## About the club

AI for Chemistry Club brings together students interested in the intersection of artificial intelligence and chemical science. We aim to promote learning, discussion and practical exploration of AI approaches in chemistry, while encouraging connections across mathematics, physics, biology and AI itself. Through interdisciplinary exchange and peer mentoring, we aim to help students learn from one another and use AI tools responsibly in scientific research and learning.

A student-run Department-associated Group supported by the Department of Chemistry at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology.

中文核对要点：社团是学生主导、化学系支持的学术社群；化学是基础，同时关注数学、物理、生物学与 AI 本身。学习、实践、跨学科交流、同伴互助和负责任使用 AI 是目标，不宣称已有成果或成型制度。

### AI × Chemistry
